Know the boundaries

Works in this preview

  • Three deterministic, built-in timelines
  • Setup-once, timed repetition, and consistent restart
  • Serial text and logical LED on Uno / ESP32 teaching profiles
  • Fictional network and text frames in the ESP32 story

Not supported

  • Custom C++, compilation, or firmware execution
  • Hardware timing, memory, interrupts, sensors, or libraries
  • Real networking, peripherals, or physical devices
  • ESP8266, ESP32-S3, Nano, or other target emulation

The browser clock drives a storyboard, not a CPU. Long suspension (over 60 seconds) stops with a diagnostic rather than inventing missed output.
